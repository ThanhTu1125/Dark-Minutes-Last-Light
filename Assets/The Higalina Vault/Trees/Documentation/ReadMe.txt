The Higalina Vault - Trees

This asset pack contains 100 unique fantasy tree designs (200 total Unity Prefabs, with and without shadows) with transparent backgrounds.
Ideal for top-down & isometric RPGs, farming & survival crafting games, RTS strategy titles, overworld level design, and Virtual Tabletop (VTT) map creation.

Included:
- 200 Ready-to-use Unity Prefabs (100 unique trees x 2 variants: with & without shadow)
- Pre-configured SpriteRenderers and custom 2D Colliders for instant drag-and-drop use
- High-resolution PNG files with transparent backgrounds
- Multiple seasonal variations (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter, Bare/Dead)
- Magical, corrupted, and biome-specific fantasy tree variants
- Clean, organized Unity Demo Scene for fast preview and selection
